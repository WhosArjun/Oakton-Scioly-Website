# Oakton-Scioly-Website
Oakton Science Olympiad Website
Oakton Science Olympiad has never had a website. Hopefully this website can bring more outreach to the club, as we are looking for sponsors. Our team
is very passionate and has lots of potential, we got the national bid @ USC but couldn't go due to logistics and funding. With a website we are looking
to change this so we can achieve more in the future.
  The website includes 7 tabs : 
      Teams : Members of each team, including the captains. The members are split into 3 teams of 15. Solar (Best), Lunar (Second Best), Eclipse (Third Best)
      Captains : A funny, short description that speaks a lot about the captains. Even though they are captains with lots of responsibility they are still
      extremely friendly.
      Achievements : These include our teams placing into two categories : Invitationals and Regionals/States competitions. We have placed numerous times at 
      invitationals, and succesfully qualified for states for a huge amount of consecutive years. This tab also includes the specific placings of each of the 
      teams in their respective events.
      Blog : The blog includes updates on each of the competitions, and talks about the performance of the team. This includes how well the team did, what they
      learned, and how the overall placements went. Including some of the highs by the individual members of each team. 
      Tryouts : The tryouts section will include what you need to know for tryouts, talks about what the club is about in general. Also providing some resources 
      that you can use to study for the tryouts. The tryouts information such as dates are included in the site. It talks about the dates of some of the invitational
      and regional/state competitions.
      Resources : These include the resources that we use to study as a team. It also includes some old practice tests, and the science olympiad organizations that
      you might want to use to plan out different competitions. Also allows you to visit (Duosmium) the official site of science olympiad results. Which can tell 
      your team which events you might want to improve on.
      About : The about section talks about the build and writing events in general. It also talks about our personal team culture at Oakton Science Olympiad.

Hopefully this website brings lots of sponsors and new aspiring members. Go OAKTON SCIENCE OLYMPIAD!
