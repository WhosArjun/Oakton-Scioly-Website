Boyceville Satellite Invitational
December 1, 2025 · Online satellite format
Solar, 1st: Rocks & Minerals Solar, 3rd: Dynamic Planet Solar, 4th: Codebusters Lunar, 3rd: Dynamic Planet

Boyceville is a satellite invitational, so every school runs the test blocks at its own site and the scores get compiled online afterward. It's a low-key way to start the season before the in-person invitationals ramp up.

Solar took 1st in Rocks & Minerals and added a 3rd in Dynamic Planet and a 4th in Codebusters.

Lunar picked up a 3rd in Dynamic Planet as well, a solid first result for a team still finding its footing this early in the season.

Nothing dramatic, but a decent baseline heading into Flint Hill two weeks later.

🏔
Flint Hill Invitational
December 13, 2025 · Flint Hill School, Oakton VA
Solar, 1st overall of 32 5 first-place events Lunar, 11th overall

Solar finished 1st overall out of 32 teams at Flint Hill, the first time an Oakton Scioly team has won an invitational outright.

Solar took first place in five events: Astronomy, Chemistry Lab, Codebusters, Machines, and Remote Sensing, along with a 2nd in Entomology and a 2nd in Dynamic Planet.

Lunar finished 11th of 32, led by a 1st in Dynamic Planet and a 2nd in Codebusters.

Good way to open the invitational season, and it set expectations for the rest of the winter.

🏛️
Fairfax Invitational
January 10, 2026 · Fairfax High School, 70 teams
Solar, 2nd overall of 70 8 first-place events Eclipse, 3rd: Hovercraft

Fairfax drew 70 teams, the largest field Solar has competed in this season.

Solar finished 2nd overall with eight first-place finishes: Astronomy, Chemistry Lab, Codebusters, Dynamic Planet, Experimental Design, Machines, Remote Sensing, and Rocks & Minerals, plus a 2nd in Boomilever and a 3rd in Helicopter.

Eclipse took 3rd in Hovercraft, a solid build result in that size of a field.

Close to 1st, but a strong showing against a much bigger and tougher field than usual.

🦇
Virginia Tech Invitational
January 31, 2026 · Virginia Tech, Blacksburg VA
Solar, 4th overall of 32 3 first-place events Eclipse, 2nd: Entomology Lunar, 12th overall

Virginia Tech is usually one of the tougher fields on the schedule. Solar finished 4th of 32.

Solar took 1st in Codebusters, Entomology, and Rocks & Minerals, with a 2nd in Circuit Lab, a 2nd in Dynamic Planet, a 2nd in Experimental Design, and a 2nd in Helicopter.

Eclipse had its best invitational so far: 2nd in Entomology and a 4th in Hovercraft.

Lunar finished 12th overall, led by a 4th place in Anatomy & Physiology.

Long drive to Blacksburg, but a good tune-up before regionals.

🎓
Princeton Invitational
February 7, 2026 · Princeton University, NJ
3rd: Dynamic Planet 3rd: Rocks & Minerals Top 10 in 7 events

Princeton draws teams from up and down the East Coast, and it's a step up in competition from most of the other invitationals on the schedule.

Solar placed 3rd in Dynamic Planet and 3rd in Rocks & Minerals, with a 4th in Helicopter and a 6th in Codebusters against a much deeper field than usual.

No firsts this time, but a solid showing against tougher out-of-state competition heading into regionals.

🏆
Virginia Regional Tournament @ UMW
February 28, 2026 · University of Mary Washington, Fredericksburg VA
Solar, 6th overall 2 first-place events Qualified for states Lunar, 3rd: Entomology

Regionals at UMW was the official Virginia qualifier for states.

Solar qualified with a 6th place finish, led by 1st in Codebusters and 1st in Rocks & Minerals, plus a 2nd in Entomology.

Lunar finished 21st, with a 3rd in Entomology as the highlight.

Eclipse put up a 7th in Engineering CAD and an 8th in Anatomy & Physiology.

Solar's finish locked in a spot at states three weeks later.

🥈
Virginia State Tournament @ UVA
March 21, 2026 · University of Virginia, Charlottesville VA
2nd in Virginia 4 first-place events Best finish in Oakton history

Solar finished 2nd at Virginia States, the best result an Oakton Scioly team has posted at states.

Solar won four events: Rocks & Minerals, Codebusters, Entomology, and Water Quality.

Also podiumed in Chemistry Lab (2nd), Dynamic Planet (2nd), Machines (2nd), Remote Sensing (2nd), Experimental Design (3rd), and Materials Science (3rd).

Thanks to everyone who put in the hours this season, officers, members, coaches, and parents.

Tryout Information

Interested in joining Oakton Science Olympiad? Here's everything you need to know about tryouts for the 2026-2027 season. We welcome students of all backgrounds; a passion for science and a willingness to put in the work matter far more than experience.

📚 Tryout Guide ✍️ Tryout Sign-Up Form
🎉
Interest meeting: September 9, 2026 Curious about Scioly but not sure yet? Come to our interest meeting to meet the officers, hear about the season, and get your questions answered. Time and room TBD, check Instagram @oaktonscioly and your school email closer to the date.
📅
Tryout dates: September 28 – October 2, 2026 Keep an eye on our Instagram @oaktonscioly and check your school email for exact times and location.
🧬
Who can try out?

Any currently enrolled Oakton High School student in grades 9 through 12 is eligible to try out. No prior Science Olympiad experience is required.

📋
What to expect
  • A written science knowledge test covering biology, chemistry, physics, and earth science
  • An optional build and engineering challenge component
  • A short conversation with the captains
  • Results announced within one to two weeks
📚
How to prepare
  • Read our full Tryout Guide for details on format and scoring
  • Browse the Scioly.org wiki for event overviews
  • Review current Science Olympiad events on soinc.org
  • Practice with past tests from scioly.org/tests
  • Brush up on general STEM concepts from your classes
🏗️
Interested in build events?

If engineering events like helicopter, hovercraft, or boomilever interest you, mention it at tryouts. Prior experience with building, robotics, or engineering helps, but it's not required.

🤝
Team structure

Oakton Scioly runs three teams: Solar, Lunar, and Eclipse. Each team competes in invitationals and the regional tournament, and our top team advances to states.

💬
Questions?

Reach out on Instagram @oaktonscioly or email us at ohssciol@gmail.com. Our captains are happy to answer questions and help you prepare. Don't be shy, we were all new once.

⏳
Time commitment

Science Olympiad is a real commitment, but an incredibly rewarding one. Here's a rough breakdown of what to expect:

  • Practices: one weekly team meeting, plus studying with your event partners outside of school
  • Invitationals: we sign up for several through the winter, on top of regionals and states if we qualify; nationals is the long-term goal
  • Regionals: one full weekend day in late February
  • States (Solar only): one full day in March at a Virginia university
  • Build events: require extra time outside of meetings to build and test devices

About Oakton Scioly

Oakton Science Olympiad is a student-led STEM competition team focused on science, engineering, inquiry, and teamwork.

🧪
Who we are

Oakton Scioly competes in invitationals, regional tournaments, and the Virginia State Tournament every season. Our members work across biology, chemistry, physics, engineering, earth science, and coding events.

🏆
Achievements

During the 2025-2026 season, Solar placed 2nd at Virginia States, the best finish in Oakton Scioly history. Our team consistently places at top-tier invitationals like Princeton and Virginia Tech.

⚙️
Build events

Members design and test engineering devices including helicopters, electric vehicles, hovercrafts, boomilevers, and robot systems.

📖
Study events

Members study everything from the earth sciences (Entomology, Dynamic Planet, Water Quality) to the human body (Anatomy) and beyond (Astronomy). Members build binders and reference sheets for competition.

🌌
Team culture

Beyond competing, Oakton Scioly is a community built on collaboration, late-night studying on Discord, inside jokes, snacks, and teamwork.
