About Oakton Scioly

Oakton Science Olympiad is a student-led STEM competition team focused on science, engineering, inquiry, and teamwork.

🧪
Who We Are

Oakton Scioly competes in invitationals, regional tournaments, and the Virginia State Tournament every season. Our members work across biology, chemistry, physics, engineering, earth science, and coding events. These events require constant studying, immense passion and focus.

🏆
Achievements

During the 2025–2026 season, Solar placed 2nd at Virginia States, the best finish in Oakton Scioly history. Our team has consistently been placing at top tier invitationals such as Princeton and VTech.

⚙️
Build Events

Members design and test engineering devices including helicopters, electric vehicles, hovercrafts, boomilevers, and robot systems.

📖
Study Events

Members study events from Earth Sciences (Entomology, Dynamic Planet, Water Quality) to the human body (Anatomy), outside of this world (Astronomy). In these events members will build binders and cheat sheets to help them during the test at the competition.

🌌
Team Culture

Beyond competing, Oakton Scioly is a community built on collaboration, late-night studying (on discord), inside jokes, snacks, and teamwork. We love one another very much, its a second family to all of us.
